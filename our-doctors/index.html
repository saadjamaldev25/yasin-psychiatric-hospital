Dr Shafiq Ghulam Yasin

Clinical Director and Lead Consultant Psychiatrist

Dr Shafiq Yasin graduated from Dow Medical College in 1994 and has worked in psychiatry since 1996 and as a consultant psychiatrist since 2001. His clinical experience spans Pakistan, Ireland, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand and Saudi Arabia, including senior consultant work in Jeddah.

His father, the late Dr Ghulam Yasin, founded Yasin Psychiatric Hospital. Working alongside him shaped Dr Shafiq’s interest in reducing stigma and helping families recognise mental health concerns early.

  • MBBS, Dow Medical College, 1994
  • Diploma in Psychological Medicine (DPM), Dublin, Ireland, 2000
  • Member of the Royal College of Psychiatrists (MRCPsych), United Kingdom, 2001
  • Fellow of the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Psychiatrists (FRANZCP), 2007

His clinical interests include adult psychiatry, mood disorders, addiction, intellectual disability and mental health in older adults. He is the author of Mental Health Awareness (2013) and Mental Health Prevention (2014).

Dr Shafiq’s approach to medication

When medication may help, Dr Shafiq explains the expected benefits, possible side effects and available options. The plan takes into account your health, prior experiences with medication and preferences. You’ll agree on what to monitor and how to raise concerns or review the plan.
