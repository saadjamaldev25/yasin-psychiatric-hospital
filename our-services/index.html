Outpatient and specialist services

  • Initial psychiatric assessment, treatment planning, medication management and follow-up.
  • Psychological assessment, psychotherapy, couples therapy and speech therapy.
  • Specialist consultations, second opinions, clinical reports and review of relevant laboratory results.
  • Video consultations when suitable for your clinical needs.

Inpatient, emergency and pharmacy services

We provide inpatient admission, psychiatric nursing and rehabilitation for alcohol and other substance use disorders. Our psychiatric emergency department assesses urgent mental health concerns. The on-site pharmacy dispenses prescribed medicines and can answer questions about their use.

Conditions we assess and treat

  • Depression, persistent depressive disorder (dysthymia), bipolar disorder and perinatal or postpartum mood disorders.
  • Anxiety, panic attacks, social anxiety, OCD and post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD).
  • Schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders, personality disorders, self-harm and suicide risk.
  • ADHD in children, adolescents and adults, autism spectrum and other developmental concerns.
  • Sleep disorders, eating disorders, dementia and other cognitive disorders.
  • Alcohol and other substance use disorders.

Treatments and physical health monitoring

The hospital offers electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) and cranial stimulation therapy (CST) following specialist assessment. Services also include ECG, cardiac monitoring and physical health checks such as blood pressure, oxygen saturation, random blood glucose and weight. We record baseline measurements and repeat checks as appropriate to your care.
