Appointments, fees and follow-up

Find out how to book a psychiatrist appointment in Karachi, confirm consultation fees and prepare for your first visit in Nazimabad.

Your first psychiatric assessment

Your clinician will ask about your current concerns, when they started and how they affect your life. They will also review your medical and treatment history. If you can, bring a list of your medicines and any relevant prescriptions or reports. The consultation is private and your health information is handled confidentially, subject to applicable legal and safety obligations.

At the end of the visit, your clinician will share their initial assessment and discuss treatment options. A first follow-up is often arranged within one to three weeks. Later appointments are scheduled according to your needs, progress and medication monitoring requirements.

Consultation fees and financial support

Fees vary by clinician and appointment type. Reception can confirm the current charge for an in-person or video consultation. Reduced fees may be available for people experiencing financial hardship. Limited Zakat assistance is subject to eligibility and availability. Contact reception to ask about assessment for financial support.

Between appointments

Contact reception if you have an important concern between visits. Routine treatment questions and medication changes are usually discussed during an appointment. Extended or frequent telephone consultations may incur a fee.

Keep your planned follow-up appointments so your clinician can review your progress and medication. Request a review before your supply runs low. If you miss an appointment or are overdue for review, contact reception to arrange your next visit.
