Yasin Psychiatric Hospital

Our history and approach to care

The late Dr Ghulam Yasin founded Yasin Psychiatric Hospital in Karachi in 1970. Today, Clinical Director Dr Shafiq Ghulam Yasin and the hospital team continue that work through psychiatric assessment, psychological care, inpatient treatment and support for families.

Our vision

We envision a community where people of all ages can seek mental health care without stigma and are heard, understood and treated with dignity.

Our mission

To provide compassionate, evidence-based mental health care through careful assessment, individualised treatment and coordinated follow-up. We listen to patients and work with them and their families to make informed decisions about care.

How our team works

Our team includes professionals with international qualifications and training. Psychiatrists, psychologists, nurses, pharmacists and other clinicians coordinate care as needed. We draw on clinical evidence and consider your symptoms, physical health, daily responsibilities and preferences when recommending treatment.

Our psychiatrists assess mental health symptoms, prescribe medication when appropriate and request or review relevant tests. Psychologists provide assessment and therapy within their scope of practice. With your agreement, your care team can coordinate with other clinicians involved in your treatment.
