Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions from patients and families.

I’m sensitive to medication. What can I do?

Tell your clinician about past reactions and everything you take, including supplements and over-the-counter medicines. Depending on the medicine, a lower starting dose, an alternative treatment or closer monitoring may help.

How long will I need medication?

It depends on your condition, how you respond and your history. If you want to stop or change your medication, talk to your prescriber first so you can plan it safely.

What if medication makes me feel drowsy or slowed down?

Contact your clinician promptly if this happens. They can review your dose, timing and treatment options.

What should I do with unused or expired medicines?

Ask our pharmacy whether it can accept them or advise you on safe disposal.

How often will I need follow-up appointments?

A first review is often scheduled within one to three weeks. After that, the timing depends on your symptoms, treatment and monitoring needs.

How do I request a prescription refill?

Discuss refills at your review. If you are running low, call reception during business hours. Your clinician may need to see you before renewing the prescription.

Can I get a refill if I missed my appointment?

Call reception to rebook. If you have run out and need advice before your next visit, ask about an urgent review or contact your regular doctor.

What if I’m running late or need to cancel?

Please call reception as soon as you can. Depending on the schedule, we may be able to see you later that day or help you rebook. Ask reception about the current missed-appointment policy.

How can I contact Dr Shafiq Yasin?

Call or WhatsApp reception on 0330 7997999 for appointments and routine enquiries. In an emergency, go to the nearest emergency department or call local emergency services.

Can I have a video appointment or request medicine delivery?

Ask reception if video consultation is appropriate for your visit. For prescription delivery, ask the pharmacy about availability, eligible medicines and delivery charges.
